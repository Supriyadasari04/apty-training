Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower, or La Tour Eiffel, is one of the most recognizable structures in the world. Located in Paris, France, it was completed in 1889 as the entrance arch to the World's Fair. Standing at 330 meters tall, it was the tallest man-made structure for 41 years.

Where Is the Eiffel Tower?

The Eiffel Tower is located on the Champ de Mars in the 7th arrondissement of Paris, beside the Seine River. It attracts over 7 million visitors annually and is a symbol of French culture and innovation.
