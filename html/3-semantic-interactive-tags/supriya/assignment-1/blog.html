Movie Overview

Interstellar is a 2014 science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan. It explores themes of love, sacrifice, and the survival of humanity through space exploration.

Themes and Motifs

The film delves into complex themes such as time dilation, the power of love, and the human spirit's resilience in the face of adversity.

Main Characters

  • Cooper - A former NASA pilot who embarks on a mission to save humanity.
  • Murph - Cooper's daughter, who plays a crucial role in solving the gravitational equation.
  • TARS - A robot companion that assists the crew on their journey.

Director's Vision

Christopher Nolan blends hard science with emotional storytelling in Interstellar. His collaboration with physicist Kip Thorne ensures the film’s scientific accuracy, especially around black holes and time dilation.

Fun Facts

  • The black hole "Garguntua" is scientifically accurate and required 800 terabytes of data to render.
  • Nolan planted 500 acres of corn for filming - and later sold it for profit!
