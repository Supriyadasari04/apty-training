Introduction

Coffee is not just a beverage—it's a culture, a ritual, and a daily necessity for millions around the globe. From strong espresso shots to frothy cappuccinos, coffee brings people together and fuels our day.

Popular Types of Coffee

  • Espresso – The base of many coffee drinks, rich and intense.
  • Latte – Espresso with steamed milk, smooth and creamy.
  • Americano – Espresso diluted with hot water for a lighter taste.
  • Cappuccino – Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and foam.
  • Cold Brew – Coffee brewed cold for a smoother, less acidic flavor.

Health Benefits of Coffee

Coffee can be more than a morning boost. When consumed in moderation, it may offer several health benefits:

  • Rich in antioxidants
  • May improve brain function
  • Boosts metabolism
  • Reduces risk of certain diseases like Parkinson’s and Type 2 Diabetes

Coffee Around the World

Every country has its own coffee culture. Italians enjoy their espresso standing at a bar. Turkish coffee is strong and thick. In Sweden, "Fika" is a daily ritual of coffee and pastries with friends. Ethiopian coffee ceremonies can last hours and are rich in tradition.

Did You Know?

The word "coffee" comes from the Arabic word qahwa, which originally meant wine. Coffee was discovered in Ethiopia and became popular in the Middle East before spreading across the globe.
