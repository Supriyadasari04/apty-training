Hyderabadi Biryani

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice – 2 cups (soaked for 30 mins)
  • Chicken – 500 grams
  • Yogurt – 1 cup
  • Fried Onions – 1 cup
  • Mint & Coriander Leaves – chopped
  • Biryani Masala – 2 tbsp
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste – 1 tbsp
  • Saffron – soaked in warm milk
  • Ghee and Oil – as needed
  • Salt – to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, masala, salt, and ginger-garlic paste. Let it rest for 1–2 hours.
  2. Cook rice 80% and drain. Set aside.
  3. In a deep pot, add oil and layer chicken, then rice, fried onions, and chopped herbs.
  4. Repeat layers, drizzle saffron milk, ghee, and seal the pot tightly.
  5. Cook on low flame (dum) for 25–30 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with raita or mirchi ka salan.
