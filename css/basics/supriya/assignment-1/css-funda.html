Explore Bangalore

India’s tech capital and garden city

Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is the capital of Karnataka and India’s leading IT hub. Known as the Silicon Valley of India, it hosts the headquarters of major tech giants like Infosys, Wipro, and Flipkart. The city is famous for its pleasant climate, lush gardens, diverse culture, and vibrant startup ecosystem. It is also a major center for education, research, and aerospace industries.

Top Attractions

  • Lalbagh Botanical Garden
  • Bangalore Palace
  • Cubbon Park
  • ISKCON Temple
  • Bannerghatta National Park
